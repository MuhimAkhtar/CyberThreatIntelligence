1. Executive Summary

The National Cyber Threat Intelligence Platform (CTP) is an enterprise-grade threat intelligence aggregation, incident response, and digital forensics platform. Built with event-driven NestJS microservices, Apache Kafka streaming, Elasticsearch indexing, and an interactive Cyber Command Center UI, it provides real-time threat feed ingestion, automated playbook execution, SIEM log forwarding (Splunk & Wazuh), AI incident report synthesis via Kimi 3 on Modal Cloud, and transactional email alerting.
